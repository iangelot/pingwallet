06 / THE IMPORTANT BITS

Good to
know.

Clear answers, before you connect anything.

Does PingWallet need my private key or seed phrase?

No. PingWallet only reads public blockchain data. It never asks for a private key, seed phrase, or permission to move funds.

What chains can I monitor?

Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Robinhood Chain, and Tron. EVM addresses can be followed across supported EVM networks or limited to one.

How fast are alerts?

Alerts are typically delivered within seconds after a transaction confirms. Network conditions can affect timing.

Can I send alerts to a group?

Yes. Plans include group support, with paid tiers allowing more groups. Add PingWallet to your Telegram group and route the wallets you choose.

How does the 7-day money-back guarantee work?

Try a paid plan for seven days. If it is not useful, message support on Telegram and request a refund.
